Phone placement
  1. Off to one side or below, about 1 m from the board, out of the line of fire.
  2. At an angle of roughly 20–45°, not straight on. Darts read best when you can see their length.
  3. Fill the frame. Get close or zoom so the board is large. Keep the phone dead still.
  4. Even light with soft shadows. A ring light around the board is ideal.
